# Code of Conduct

OpenVysor is for everyone. We want taking part to be welcoming and free of harassment,
whatever your experience, background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Keep feedback about the work, and make it constructive.
- Accept correction gracefully, and help newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks and deliberate disruption.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may block anyone who breaks these
rules. To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer through their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/SahinurDEV). Reports will be handled in confidence.
